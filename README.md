Project Title: The Impact of Generative AI on the Labor Market

Research Overview: This project investigates the impact of AI on the labor market, focusing on the relationship between generative AI capabilities and occupational wage in South Korea. All occupational information used in this study was obtained from Workpedia, including job descriptions, skill requirements, and occupational wage data. The study constructs an occupation-level AI exposure measure using GPT-5 and examines its relationship with labor market outcomes.

Data: The study uses occupational information and wage data from South Korean employment databases.
The dataset includes:
Occupational descriptions
Job-specific skill requirements
AI time-saving scores (0.00–1.00) 
Average monthly wages (2023–2025)
Job posting wages (2023–2025)

Methodology: GPT-5 is used to estimate the potential proportion of working time that could be saved through generative AI assistance. Each occupation is evaluated based on its job description and skill requirements. The GPT-5 prompt is provided in the Python script.
